                Free GPU for games
                When a fullscreen app (a game or a video player) is in front for 5 seconds, Whisper and the AI model are unloaded, as with the Free GPU hotkey. Meanwhile a dictation loads only Whisper and is pasted without AI cleanup, and Edit mode is off. The models load again 30 seconds after the game is closed, minimised or no longer fullscreen, not when you just use another window or monitor. Browsers, calls (Teams, Zoom, Discord) and remote desktops in fullscreen do not count.
                Freed for a game: dictations run without AI cleanup until it ends.
              

              

                
              

            

            

              

                Unload when idle
                On mains power, unload Whisper and the AI model after this long without a dictation, to free the graphics card. The next dictation loads them again and takes a few seconds longer. On battery it is always 10 minutes.
