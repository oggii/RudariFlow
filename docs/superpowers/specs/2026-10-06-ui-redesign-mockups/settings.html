What goes where (both options)
  • Dictation — all hotkeys, hold/toggle, microphone, start/stop sounds. Advanced: Free GPU + meeting hotkeys, mute other apps, "Send it".
  • AI cleanup — on/off, style, Write in, Edit mode, rules per app. Advanced: AI model choice (E4B/E2B), "Try it" test box.
  • Dictionary — your words and your replacements on one page with search; suggestions from corrections; Swiss spelling.
  • Models & GPU — speech model with download state, language. Advanced: local/cloud engine, GPU backend, PC check, Free GPU for games, unload when idle, unused models.
  • General — display language, start with Windows, history (keep text / audio / off), version.
