# RudariFlow 0.16.0 UI audit: facts and options

Read-only audit of `E:\claude\RudariFlow` (branch `main`, 84447e5). Nothing in the repo, the installed app or its data was touched; the real app was not started.

## How this was measured

- The frontend was copied to `ui-audit\app`, built there with Vite, and rendered in headless Chromium 153 (Playwright 1.63) with a mock of `window.__TAURI_INTERNALS__` injected before the app scripts. The bundle hashes equal the repo's `dist\` (`main-L7xgbyA_.js`), so this is the 0.16.0 frontend.
- Two data sets: **populated** (12 dictionary words, 2 suggestions, 3 replacements, 3 app rules, 8 history entries, 2 meetings, 6 sounds in 3 categories, both models downloaded, AI ready) and **first run** (`Settings::default()`, nothing downloaded, all lists empty).
- Sizes: 1600×900 and 900×600. 900×600 is both the default and the minimum window (`src-tauri\tauri.conf.json:15-18`).
- Numbers are in `metrics.json`, `probe.json`, `names.json`; scripts in `harness\`.
- Limits: this is Chromium, not the WebView2 window, so the sidebar finding (problem 2) deserves a 1-minute check in the real app. Control counts depend on the sample data. The daily / set-once / expert split is my judgment. The first-run failure path (problem 1) is read from the code, not observed.

## (b) Top 10 clarity problems, by impact

**1. There is no first-run path, and the app says "Ready" when it is not.**
A new user lands on General: four set-once settings, none of which starts a dictation (`firstrun-general-en-900x600-fold.png`). The sidebar pill is green "Ready" although no Whisper model is downloaded; it only knows Ready / Recording / Transcribing (`main.ts:634-656`). The dictation hotkey (Ctrl+Shift+Space, `settings.rs:199`) is shown only on the third tab. The Download button is row 5 of Engine, labelled "Model Size", with its select text cut to "Small (~466 MB) — r" (`firstrun-engine-en-1600x900.png`). Dictating without a model returns "Whisper model not found" to a log line only (`recorder.rs:943`, `main.rs:2907-2908`); the overlay has notices for no speech, a missing microphone and failed edits, but none for a missing model, so by the code nothing tells the user why no text appeared. No onboarding, welcome or checklist exists in `src\` or `index.html`.

**2. Ten flat sidebar items, and at the default window size the tenth is not reachable.**
The list mixes settings (General, Engine, Recording, AI cleanup), word data (Dictionary, Replacements), a log (History) and three tools (Files, Meetings, Soundboard) without grouping (`index.html:26-99`). At 900×600 "Soundboard" sits at y 600-633 and the footer at 637-699, below the window; the sidebar does not scroll (`body { overflow: hidden }`, `style.css:40`; a wheel test moved nothing). The item needs a window 633 px high, the footer 699 px. Cause: the logo is a 1024×1024 PNG shown at 160×160 whose visible mark is 117×27 px with 67 px of empty space above and below, making the header 223 px tall (`style.css:89-94`, `probe.json`). Evidence: any `*-900x600-fold.png`.

**3. Status is scattered and the one global indicator misses most states.**
The sidebar pill does not show: model missing, models unloaded (Free GPU, idle, game), AI state, a meeting recording, a file transcribing, a download. While a meeting records it still reads "Ready" (`meetings-recording-en-900x600-fold.png`). AI state is only on the AI tab, "freed for a game" only on Engine and AI, the Whisper download is a 3 px bar without numbers on Engine (`index.html:234-236`) while the AI download shows "x / y GB" (`ai-settings.ts:437`). The pill is not a live region (`names.json`). The overlay pill is the best status surface, but it takes its language from Windows, not from the Display Language setting (`overlay.html:359-362`).

**4. Engine buries the two settings everyone needs among GPU plumbing.**
Model and Language share one undivided list with GPU Backend, PC check, Free GPU for games and Unload when idle: 7 rows under one heading. 86 % of the page's characters are grey hint text; one hint is 470 characters. The page is 1.96 screens at 900×600 (`engine-en-900x600.png`). The labels are implementation words: "Engine", "Transcription backend", "GPU Backend", "flash attention", "CUDA", "Vulkan". The selected model reads "Large v3 Turbo q8 (~870 MB" because the text needs 451 px and has 166 px (490 / 130 px in German); the ✓ that says "downloaded" is cut off with it.

**5. Things that belong together are on different tabs.**
- Language: five controls in four places (General › Display Language, Engine › Language, AI cleanup › Write in, AI rules › language per app, Files › Language).
- Freeing the GPU: hotkey on Recording (`index.html:332-343`), automatic for games and when idle on Engine (`246-272`), status on AI cleanup.
- Models: Whisper on Engine ("Model Size"), AI on AI cleanup ("Model"), speaker model implicit in Files › Speakers, deleting on Engine.
- Hotkeys: five on Recording (including the meeting one), two plus one per sound on Soundboard.
- Editing: Edit mode on AI cleanup, its "Rewrite last dictation" hotkey on Recording.
- Spelling: Swiss spelling on Dictionary, while the AI instructions placeholder suggests typing the same rule (`index.html:540`).
- Microphone: General, and again under Soundboard › Devices.
- Naming: nav says "Files", the page says "Transcribe a file"; the General icon is a clock, as is History's.

**6. The hotkeys, the core of the product, have no overview.**
All five live as rows 2-6 of Recording between a mode switch and a voice command. The main one is labelled only "Hotkey"; two are unset by default. Nothing on the first screen, in the sidebar or in the tray menu shows them. An unset key renders as a two-line "Not / set" box (`recording-en-1600x900.png`). The capture flow itself is good.

**7. Soundboard puts 850 px of set-once settings before the sounds whenever it is one column.**
In windows narrower than about 1,120 px (content area under 900 px, so in the default window and always in the pop-out) the order is settings, Devices, hint, then the library: the toolbar starts at y 847 and the first sound at y 938 of 562 visible (`soundboard-en-900x600.png`, `probe.json`). The pop-out, the surface meant for use during a game, opens on settings and is 3.0 screens tall (`soundboard-popout-en-460x680.png`). The page carries 70 controls with 6 sounds; every sound shows 8-9 controls permanently. 25 controls depend on a tooltip for their name or explanation.

**8. Help text outweighs the controls and is low-contrast.**
64 help texts are visible across the ten pages; 9 are 150 characters or longer. On the settings pages 80-87 % of all characters are in the secondary colour `#6e6e76` at 12 px, which measures 3.94:1 on the background and 3.67:1 on cards (WCAG AA asks 4.5:1). The labels that carry the structure are 13 px / 500, the only larger steps are the 18 px page title and four 14 px subheadings in the whole app. Other help is tooltip-only or in dismissible boxes, so three different help patterns coexist.

**9. The lists do not scale and repeat their actions on every row.**
History: no search or grouping, 88 px per entry on average, about 19,100 px at the 200-entry cap; 8 entries produce 30 text buttons (`history-en-1600x900.png`). Dictionary: one word per 38 px row in a single column, no search, about 3,800 px for 100 words; the add field starts at y 540 of 562 at 900×600 because three switches and the suggestions come first (`dictionary-en-900x600.png`). Files: with a result, the transcript starts at y 640 of 562, below the fold (`files-result-en-900x600-fold.png`). Meetings and Soundboard have search; History and Dictionary do not.

**10. The same job looks and behaves differently from tab to tab, and the keyboard is half-supported.**
- Deleting: two-click confirm for Clear history, models, meetings and sounds; immediate and without undo for a history entry, a dictionary word, a replacement, a rule and a category (`main.ts:923`, `dictionary.ts:170`, `board.ts:428`).
- Remove is the word "Remove", an × or the word "Delete", depending on the tab. Seven different list-row designs exist.
- "Downloaded" is a disabled ✓ button on Engine and a hidden button on AI cleanup.
- Keyboard: the nav items are `<a>` without `href`, so Tab never reaches them; the first Tab stop is "by oggi" (`metrics.json`, tab order). Switches show no focus indicator at all (`style.css:245-249`); selects and inputs replace the outline with a 1 px accent border and a ring of 1.1:1.
- Names: 37 of 43 form controls on the seven older tabs have no accessible name; Meetings and Soundboard have 0 unnamed (`names.json`). Segmented buttons carry no pressed state.
- Targets below 24 px: switches 40×22, text buttons 21 px high (40-60 wide), the "No AI" checkbox 13×13.

### Smaller findings

- German: strings are 16 % longer overall. No page scrolls sideways in either language at either size. Three real defects: the Soundboard row "Tastenkürzel, das Sound-Tastenkürzel ein- und ausschaltet" has its hint running under the key box at 900 px (text to x 772, control from x 719, `soundboard-de-900x600.png`); the History meta line is ellipsised so the app name is lost (needs 253 px, has 180, `history-de-900x600-fold.png`); rule-language selects cut "Sprache wie eingestellt" (135 px in 86).
- At 1600×900 six of ten pages keep a 600 px text column in a 1,380 px area; Files, Meetings and Soundboard use the width.
- Other contrast values: version text 1.84:1, placeholders 2.11:1, nav icons 2.12:1, input borders and the off-switch track 1.41:1.
- The font is fetched from Google Fonts at every start (`index.html:7-9`): a network request in an app whose pages say "Nothing leaves your PC", and a different font when offline.
- Tray items "Show RudariFlow" and "Quit" are English only (`main.rs:3419, 3422`); the meeting item is translated.
- 13 English strings use em-dashes, six of them the long model option labels behind the truncation.

## (c) Already good, keep it

- One row anatomy (label and hint left, control right) across all 41 setting rows, and auto-save everywhere with no Save button.
- Plain, concrete wording in most hints ("Music and videos go quiet while you dictate and come back afterwards"), privacy stated where it matters, and complete English / German parity (461 / 461 keys).
- Errors that name the fix and the place ("Download a Whisper model in the Engine tab first").
- The hotkey capture: inline, Esc cancels, conflicts explained in words, mouse side buttons (`hotkey-capture.ts`).
- The two-click "armed" delete without a modal dialog.
- The AI status line with tone colours; warnings that appear only when relevant (Write in, long dictionary).
- Meetings: the recording bar, notes beside the transcript, Jump to live, hint rows with one action (`meetings-open-en-1600x900-fold.png`).
- Files: one drop zone, and a file dropped anywhere on the window works.
- Soundboard two-column layout when wide, and the pop-out idea.
- The overlay pill and its states.
- A restrained palette (one accent, three status colours), one type family, five sizes; reduced-motion respected; no horizontal overflow at 900 px in either language; an empty state for every list.
- The newer tabs (Meetings, Soundboard) already do labels, focus styles and live regions properly and can serve as the house standard.

## (d) Three redesign directions

**A. Home page plus grouped sidebar.** Effort M, risk low to medium.
Add a "Home" page as the landing page: what is ready (microphone, Whisper model, AI), the hotkeys with their current keys, the last few dictations, and on first run a short checklist (download model, try the hotkey). Keep the existing pages but put the sidebar into three labelled groups, for example Use (Home, History, Files, Meetings, Soundboard), Your words (Dictionary, Replacements, AI cleanup), Settings (General, Hotkeys, Engine), and shrink the logo block so the list fits at 600 px. Fixes problems 1, 2, 3 and 6, and part of 5. The pages themselves stay as long as they are (4, 7, 8, 9 remain). Risk: existing users relearn the order; Home needs one aggregated status, for which most events already exist (`recording-state`, `ai-status`, `game-free`, `meeting-status`), while "Whisper loaded / missing" may need a small backend addition.

**B. Simple / Advanced split inside the current pages.** Effort S to M, risk low.
Leave the ten pages and their order. On each, show only the rows most people set, and move the expert rows behind one "Advanced" disclosure per page (or one Advanced page): GPU Backend, PC check, Free GPU for games, Unload when idle, Unused models, the Free GPU hotkey, Soundboard devices and hotkey switches, Keep history, "Try it". Cut each hint to one line with a "More" expander. Fixes problems 4 and 8 and eases 7; Engine would drop from 7 rows to 3. It does not help 1, 2, 3 or 5. Risk: power users (the owner included) click more to reach GPU settings; disclosure state has to be remembered; "what is advanced" needs agreeing once.

**C. Task-based navigation.** Effort L, risk high.
Rebuild the top level around what people do: Dictate, Transcribe a file, Meetings, Soundboard, plus one Settings. "Dictate" gathers everything about dictation as sub-tabs (Hotkeys and mode, Language and model, AI cleanup and rules, Dictionary, Replacements, History); each tool keeps its own options beside it; Settings holds only app-wide things (microphone, display language, autostart, GPU and storage). Five top-level items instead of ten. Fixes problems 2, 5 and 6 most thoroughly and gives 4 a natural home. Risk: the largest relearning for current users; "Dictate" becomes a hub that needs a second navigation level; the single 785-line `index.html`, the section switcher in `main.ts:147-158` and both README files change; problems 1 and 3 still need a status surface on top.

The directions combine: B is the cheapest first step, A adds the missing entry point, C is A's grouping taken to its conclusion.
